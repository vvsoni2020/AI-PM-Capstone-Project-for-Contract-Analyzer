No liquidated damages apply to any delay caused by Customer, its personnel or its other suppliers.

## 4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
4.1 Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement. This cap does not apply to fraud or wilful misconduct.
