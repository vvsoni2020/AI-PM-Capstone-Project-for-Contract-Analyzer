# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between **Nilgiri Tea Exports Ltd**, a company with its registered office at 14 Commercial Road, Coonoor 643101, Tamil Nadu ("**Customer**"), and **AtliQ Technologies Pvt Ltd**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, CIN U72900MH2019PTC321456, with its registered office at 4th Floor, Baner Business Bay, Pune 411045, Maharashtra, India ("**Supplier**").

## 1. SERVICES
1.1 Supplier shall build a supply-chain analytics platform covering tea auctions, warehouse inventory and export shipments, in three milestones described in Schedule 1.
1.2 Schedule 1 – Milestone 1: data pipeline (8 weeks); Milestone 2: inventory and auction dashboards (6 weeks); Milestone 3: export shipment tracking (6 weeks).

## 2. FEES AND PAYMENT
2.1 Fees are INR 43,00,000 (approximately USD 52,000): 30% on Milestone 1, 35% on Milestone 2 and 35% on Milestone 3.
2.2 Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

## 3. DELAY
3.1 If a milestone is delayed for reasons attributable solely to Supplier, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the fee for the affected milestone for each full week of delay, capped at 5% of that milestone fee. No liquidated damages apply to any delay caused by Customer, its personnel or its other suppliers.

## 4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
4.1 Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement. This cap does not apply to fraud or wilful misconduct.

## 5. INDEMNITY
5.1 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims to the extent caused by its own breach of this Agreement or its own negligence.

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
6.1 On payment in full, Customer owns the deliverables created specifically for it under this Agreement. Supplier keeps its pre-existing tools, libraries and know-how and grants Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual licence to use them as built into the deliverables.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY
7.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement. These obligations bind both parties equally and survive for three (3) years.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION
8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice. Customer shall pay for all work performed up to the date of termination.

## 9. GOVERNANCE
9.1 Each party appoints a project lead. The leads meet every two weeks and record decisions in writing.
9.2 Changes to scope, timeline or fees are agreed through a written change request signed by both project leads.

## 10. ACCEPTANCE
10.1 Customer shall test each deliverable within ten (10) business days of delivery and either accept it or list the specific defects.
10.2 Supplier shall fix listed defects and redeliver; a deliverable used in production for more than ten (10) business days is deemed accepted.

## 11. PERSONNEL
11.1 Supplier staffs the services from its delivery centre in Pune, India.
11.2 Neither party shall solicit the other party's employees who worked on the services during the term and for six (6) months after, except through general advertisements.

## 12. DATA PROTECTION
12.1 Customer's data is limited to shipment, inventory and sales records of businesses; it contains no personal data of individuals.
12.2 Supplier shall keep Customer data in Customer's cloud account and delete local copies at the end of the project.

## 13. INSURANCE
13.1 Supplier maintains professional indemnity insurance of at least INR 5 crore during the term.

## 14. FORCE MAJEURE
14.1 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided it notifies the other party promptly.

## 15. NOTICES
15.1 Notices are given in writing to the addresses above or to the project leads by email.

## 16. GENERAL
16.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject.
16.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 17. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
17.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes go to arbitration in Pune under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
