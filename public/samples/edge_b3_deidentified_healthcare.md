# ANALYTICS SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between **Bluebonnet Health Analytics LLC**, a Texas limited liability company with its office at 1200 Congress Avenue, Austin, Texas 78701 ("**Customer**"), and **AtliQ Inc**, a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 101 College Road East, Suite 210, Princeton, New Jersey 08540, USA ("**Supplier**").

## 1. SERVICES
1.1 Supplier shall build county-level dashboards on hospital bed occupancy and average length of stay.
1.2 Customer shall provide only aggregated data that has been de-identified under the HIPAA Safe Harbor method (45 C.F.R. §164.514(b)(2)). No protected health information (PHI) or individual-level records will be shared with Supplier, and Supplier shall not attempt to re-identify any individual. Because no PHI is shared, the parties agree that no Business Associate Agreement is required.

## 2. FEES AND PAYMENT
2.1 Fees are USD 40,000, invoiced in two milestones.
2.2 Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.

## 3. DELAY
3.1 If a milestone is delayed for reasons attributable solely to Supplier, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the fee for the affected milestone for each full week of delay, capped at 5% of that milestone fee. No liquidated damages apply to any delay caused by Customer, its personnel or its other suppliers.

## 4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
4.1 Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement. This cap does not apply to fraud or wilful misconduct.

## 5. INDEMNITY
5.1 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims to the extent caused by its own breach of this Agreement or its own negligence.

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
6.1 On payment in full, Customer owns the deliverables created specifically for it under this Agreement. Supplier keeps its pre-existing tools, libraries and know-how and grants Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual licence to use them as built into the deliverables.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY
7.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement. These obligations bind both parties equally and survive for three (3) years.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION
8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice. Customer shall pay for all work performed up to the date of termination.
